# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Counter attendants/operators at a laundry shop. They are often mid-transaction, multitasking, using a tablet mounted at the counter, and require instant access to primary actions.

## Product Purpose
To manage the shop's machines, start and stop wash/dry cycles, track job orders, handle shift-in/out, log payments, and provide local offline-first status reporting for the laundry business.

## Brand Personality
Clean, highly efficient, extremely reliable, utility-first, operational, responsive.

## Anti-references
- Modals as a primary interface (leads to fatigue).
- Tiny font sizes and cramped layouts that are hard to tap on a tablet.
- Confusing desktop-first sidebars that waste horizontal space.
- Mixed operational and manager-level dashboards where stats compete with machines.

## Design Principles
- **Touch-First Accessibility**: Tap targets must be easily clickable (minimum 48px) with plenty of breathing room to avoid accidental clicks.
- **High-Glanceability Status**: Machine state (idle, busy, offline) must be clearly visible from several feet away using distinct visual markers and clear typography.
- **Single-Tap Actions**: The most frequent actions (starting/stopping a machine, adding dry time) should be one tap away.
- **Operational-Manager Separation**: Attendants should see only what they need to keep the shop running; configuration and analytics are tucked away under the "More" or "Reports" tabs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast colors for status indicators (with supporting text, never relying on color alone).
- Clean, readable sans-serif typography (DM Sans or Inter) at a comfortable size.
- Clearly visible active and focus states for all interactive buttons.
